A long view of a new industry.

Intor invests in one thing: the land, power and buildings that artificial intelligence runs on. We take each site from the first survey to full operation and remain responsible for it afterwards. Alongside our own projects, we are forming a network of partner funds in the United States, Canada, Europe and Dubai.

Software changes every year. The need for electricity, cooling and fibre stays. We would rather own the slow, physical layer of this industry than chase its fastest-moving parts, and we expect that layer to hold its value for a very long time.

A data center outlives several generations of the servers inside it. We plan every site with that in mind.

From an empty field to a working data center.

Most projects pass through many hands. Ours stay with one team, from the first site visit to the last service call.

Finding the land

We look only at industrial land with a signed electricity contract of 10 MW or more. Titles, permits and utility routes are checked, and the full file goes to independent lawyers, who confirm that a data center can be built and operated there.

Building it

Our engineering centre designs each facility, and long-standing construction partners build it ready to hand over. We buy every piece of equipment ourselves, from transformers and backup generators to the cooling that dense AI servers require.

Signing the tenant

By the time construction ends, the entire capacity is committed to a stable international operator for five to ten years. Each site opens with a signed contract and a known income.

Running it

We stay on as the operator. Maintenance, round-the-clock monitoring, security and access control remain our job for the life of the site.

We build one kind of building.

Modular data centers of 10 MW and more, placed where the power already exists. Tenants bring their own servers and GPUs. We provide the space, electricity, cooling and protection around them.

Six rules we keep.

Markets turn and technology moves quickly. These stay the same.

  1. i.

    Power first.

    Electricity is the scarcest input of the AI era. If 10 MW is not already under contract, we walk away.

  2. ii.

    Check everything twice.

    Zoning, title, utilities and permits are reviewed by our team, then again by independent lawyers, before any money moves.

  3. iii.

    Build in modules.

    Factory-made modules go up faster and let a site grow when its tenants are ready.

  4. iv.

    Sign before we finish.

    Capacity is contracted while the concrete is still curing, so income is settled before the doors open.

  5. v.

    One team, start to finish.

    The people who choose a site are the people who run it. Nothing gets lost between contractors.

  6. vi.

    Think in decades.

    We judge a project by how it performs in its twentieth year.
